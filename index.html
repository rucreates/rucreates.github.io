The Reality

Most restaurants are losing customers they never even knew they had.

Your food might be excellent. But if your digital presence isn't, potential customers move on before they ever walk through your door.

Slow replies losing customers

Someone DMs your restaurant and waits 90 minutes for a reply. They've already booked somewhere else. Speed is trust — and trust is revenue.

No website or an outdated one

A broken link, a missing menu, a site from 2019 — every one of these quietly turns away potential regulars before they ever visit.

Inconsistent brand hurting trust

Different logo on Instagram versus your menu, no clear voice — customers sense the disconnect even if they can't name it. Consistency builds confidence.
